Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How often do you publish new tutorials?

We publish new tutorials weekly on YouTube. Subscribe to our channel to stay updated with the latest content.

❓ Can I use your content for my classroom?

Absolutely! Our educational content is designed for classrooms. Teachers and educators are welcome to use our tutorials and guides. Please give us credit and share the channel with your students.

❓ Where can I download the code and resources?

All code, documentation, and resources are available in the Resources section of our website. You can download PDFs, source code, and circuit diagrams for free.

❓ Do you offer support for specific hardware?

We primarily focus on Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and common microcontroller boards. For hardware-specific issues, check our troubleshooting guides or contact us with details about your setup.

❓ Can I contribute content or ideas?

Yes! We love community contributions. If you have project ideas, improvements, or want to contribute tutorials, please reach out to us. We review all submissions and give credit to contributors.

❓ What's the best way to report bugs or issues?

You can report bugs directly through this contact form or on our GitHub repository. Please include as much detail as possible about the issue, including your hardware setup and what you were trying to do.
